# GHL CRM

Terminology for CRM records within a GHL sub-account.

## Language

**Business**:
A CRM organization record shown as a Company in a GHL sub-account.
_Avoid_: Agency Company

**Agency Company**:
An agency-level GHL object, distinct from a sub-account Business.

**Contact company name**:
A text value on a Contact describing its company. This value alone does not establish a relationship to a Business.

**Business association**:
The relationship linking a Contact to a Business record.
